## Brand & Style

The design system delivers an intensely focused, high-clarity environment engineered for demanding revision sessions and high-yield exam retention. Built on modern Material 3 conventions, the system strips away extraneous decoration in favor of structural clarity, precise spatial indentation, and scannable technical data cards. 

Targeting students, researchers, and professional exam candidates handling information-dense subjects (STEM, medicine, legal frameworks), the aesthetic pairs an immersive dark indigo foundation with purposeful, high-contrast semantic accents: amber to signal recall urgency and critical warnings, and emerald/mint for foundational definitions and verified retention.

The design movement balances Corporate/Modern rigor with tactile functional utility:
- Content surfaces mimic structured index cards and analytical revision sheets.
- Visual hierarchy relies on tonal nesting rather than heavy drop shadows.
- Dedicated distraction-free study views collapse navigation chrome, foregrounding technical definitions, formulas, and progressive review prompts.

## Colors

The color palette centers on high-contrast readability against low-fatigue dark backdrops, adhering to Material 3 dark-theme contrast guidelines.

- **Primary (`#1E1B4B` - Deep Midnight Indigo):** Serves as the structural anchor. Tonal derivations formulate surface containers, inactive sheet borders, and navigation backdrops.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` - Amber Flare):** Reserved for high-yield callouts, exam flash alerts, spaced-repetition streaks, and active review targets.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Mint/Emerald Core):** Dedicated to definitive answers, formula terms, mastered indices, and verified flashcards.
- **Neutral (`#0F0E1E` - Void Indigo):** The deep canvas baseline, mitigating OLED eye strain during marathon night sessions.

### Functional Roles
- **Surface Tiers:** Baseline canvas sits at `#0F0E1E`. Elevated study cards transition up through `#171536`, `#1E1B4B`, and `#2A2663` to create clear hierarchical stacks without harsh border lines.
- **Text & Content Contrast:** High-emphasis copy defaults to `#F8FAFC` (95% opacity), secondary instructional copy to `#94A3B8` (70% opacity), and structural guide strokes to `#312E81` (40% opacity).

## Typography

Typography is calibrated strictly for rapid technical comprehension and structural indexing:

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its friendly yet clean geometric structure, bringing an approachable, encouraging feel to major section titles, study deck headers, and revision milestones.
- **Body & Labels (Inter):** Highly neutral and systematic, providing maximum legibility for dense technical terminology, nested definitions, and multiple-choice options.
- **Formulas & Code (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to formulas, algorithmic logic, chemical expressions, and inline variable references to avoid character misinterpretation.

### Indentation and Hierarchy
Nested notes utilize progressive left borders with hanging indents: each sub-concept indents by `16px` and features a 1.5px vertical guide rule colored with the parent level's thematic token.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout relies on a content-first responsive grid optimized for Android handhelds, foldables, and tablets:

- **Mobile (< 600dp):** 4-column fluid layout with `16px` (`1rem`) gutters and canvas margins. Single-column index stacks prioritize vertical rhythm and thumb-reach zones.
- **Tablet (600dp – 1024dp):** 8-column layout. Adapts to split-view navigation: index explorer on the left (3 columns), active flashcard/study pane on the right (5 columns).
- **Desktop / Large Foldables (> 1024dp):** 12-column layout capped at 1280px max-width to protect line-length readability (maximum 68–75 characters per line).

### Distraction-Free Study Mode
When switching to focus study mode, side margins expand inward dynamically to enforce centered reading columns (max 640px), hiding navigation bars, floating action triggers, and global status tallies.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy operates through Material 3 surface container tiers and low-contrast perimeter rules rather than deep drop shadows:

- **Canvas (Level 0):** Pure dark neutral (`#0F0E1E`), non-interactive ground.
- **Surface Low (Level 1):** Subtly tinted container (`#171536`), utilized for deck groupings and list containers.
- **Surface Medium (Level 2):** Revision cards and actionable definition items (`#1E1B4B`) equipped with a subtle `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Surface High (Level 3):** Modals, formula sheets, and expanded flashcards (`#2A2663`) sporting a soft ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`, tinted with deep indigo.

Interactive states (press, focus) never lift significantly off the z-axis; instead, they trigger subtle edge lighting (1px outline transition to secondary amber or tertiary mint) and micro-scale feedback.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry:
- Standard interactive elements (buttons, text fields, chips) adopt an `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius.
- Cards, study sheets, and expandable index panels adopt `16px` (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`).
- Full-screen modal sheets, hero study decks, and focal dialogue surfaces adopt `24px` (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`).

This level maintains a friendly, modern mobile feel while preserving internal rectangular screen area for dense multi-line mathematical formulas, definition tables, and hierarchical tree maps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** High-emphasis solid fill (`#F59E0B` with `#0F0E1E` text). `8px` radius, `44px` minimum tap height. Used for "Start Session," "Reveal Answer," and "Submit."
- **Secondary / Tonal:** `#1E1B4B` surface with `1px` border of `#312E81` and `#F8FAFC` label.
- **Tertiary (Mastery):** `#10B981` background or ghost variant used exclusively to indicate full item recall or positive self-assessment.

### Tag Chips
- **Category & Topic Tags:** Compact `28px` height, `8px` corner radius. Filled with `#171536`, framed by a `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Label styled using `label-md`.
- **Status Chips:** Inset with an indicator dot: Amber for "Pending Review," Mint for "Mastered," Slate for "New."

### Cards (Definition & Index Cards)
- Grounded on Surface Medium (`#1E1B4B`) with `16px` border radius and `16px` internal padding.
- Employs a structured layout: tag chips along the top header, clear headline in `Plus Jakarta Sans`, core technical definition in `Inter body-md`, and an optional collapsible bottom drawer for mnemonics and formulas.

### Input Fields
- Container filled with `#171536`, framed by a resting `1px` border of `#312E81`.
- Active focus state sharpens the outline to `2px` in amber (`#F59E0B`) with no glow to maintain crisp legibility.
- Helper labels and error messages appear in `label-sm` directly beneath the left indent.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Rounded `4px` square for checkboxes; circular for radios.
- Inactive state: `1.5px` border in `#94A3B8`.
- Selected state: Solid emerald fill (`#10B981`) with crisp white interior iconography.

### Specialized Study Components
- **Formula Block:** Monospace syntax block surrounded by a `1px` dashed border (`#312E81`) with dark surface fill (`#13112E`), featuring tap-to-copy variable breakdowns.
- **Indented Note Tree:** Progressive tree structure with continuous vertical guidelines connecting sub-bullets, supporting quick vertical scanning of legal statutes, taxonomy trees, and algorithmic proofs.
